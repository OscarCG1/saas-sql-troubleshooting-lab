# INC-005 — Payment Completed but Account Inactive

## Incident Information

**Incident ID:** INC-005  
**Priority:** P2 — Medium (simulated)  
**Customer:** DataFlow Systems  
**Affected User:** Maria Garcia  
**Category:** Billing / Subscription Management

## 1. Problem Description

DataFlow Systems reported that its subscription renewal payment had been completed, but the customer account remained inactive.

The affected user, Maria Garcia, was unable to access the SaaS platform.

The investigation focused on identifying inconsistencies between payment records, invoice status, subscription status, and account activation.

## 3. Findings

The investigation identified the following conditions:

1. The payment was recorded as completed.
2. The associated invoice was marked as paid.
3. The subscription remained expired.
4. The customer account remained inactive.
5. The affected user account remained inactive.

These findings indicate a potential inconsistency requiring further investigation.

## 4. Root Cause Analysis

A possible explanation is that the subscription renewal or account activation workflow did not update the corresponding account records after payment completion.

However, this hypothesis has not been confirmed.

Other possible explanations include:

- Payment reconciliation delays.
- Missing subscription renewal approval.
- Incorrect billing configuration.
- Failed application synchronization.
- An incomplete account activation process.

Additional application logs, payment provider events, and subscription renewal records would be required to determine the root cause.

## 5. Recommended Actions

1. Verify the payment transaction with the payment provider.
2. Confirm that the payment applies to an authorized subscription renewal.
3. Review payment reconciliation and subscription renewal logs.
4. Check the account activation workflow.
5. Escalate the issue to the billing or application support team if necessary.
6. Apply any approved correction through the supported application workflow.
7. Validate subscription, customer, and user account statuses.
8. Confirm that the affected user can access the platform.
