# INC-001 — Customer Cannot Access Account

## Incident Information

**Incident ID:** INC-001
**Priority:** P2 — Medium (simulated)
**Customer:** DataFlow Systems
**Affected User:** Maria Garcia
**Category:** Account Access
**Status:** Investigation Completed — Resolution Pending

## Problem Description

A customer reported that Maria Garcia was unable to access the SaaS application.

The support team initiated an investigation to identify potential account restrictions.

## Investigation Steps

### Step 1 — User Account Verification

An SQL query was executed to retrieve the affected user's account information.

**Finding:** The user account was marked as `inactive`.

### Step 2 — Customer Account Verification

The associated customer record was examined.

**Finding:** The customer account was also marked as `inactive`.

### Step 3 — Subscription Verification

A SQL JOIN query was executed to investigate the relationship between the user, customer and subscription records.

**Findings:**

* Subscription plan: Professional
* Subscription status: `expired`
* Subscription end date: 2026-08-31

## Root Cause Analysis

The investigation identified three conditions that may explain the reported access issue:

1. Inactive user account.
2. Inactive customer account.
3. Expired subscription.

The expired subscription is a plausible contributing factor, but the root cause has not been conclusively confirmed.

Additional application logs and access-control information would be required.

## Recommended Actions

1. Verify the customer's subscription renewal status.
2. Confirm whether account reactivation is authorized.
3. Review application access-control rules.
4. Escalate to the appropriate account administration team.
5. Validate user access after any authorized changes.

## Resolution Status

**Resolution:** Pending verification and authorization
