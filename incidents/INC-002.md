# INC-002 — Invoice Not Generated

## Incident Information

**Incident ID:** INC-002
**Priority:** P2 — Medium (simulated)
**Customer:** TechNova Solutions
**Category:** Billing / Invoice Generation
**Status:** Investigation Completed — Resolution Pending

## 1. Problem Description

TechNova Solutions reported that its October 2026 invoice was missing from the SaaS billing portal.

The customer confirmed that its subscription appeared active.

The support investigation focused on verifying the subscription status and identifying missing billing records.

## 2. Investigation

The investigation was performed using SQLite and DBeaver.

Two database tables were examined:

* `subscriptions`
* `invoices`

The `customers` table was also used to identify the affected company.

### Step 1 — Subscription Verification

The customer's subscription was examined using SQL JOIN operations.

**Findings:**

* Subscription plan: Enterprise
* Subscription status: `active`
* Subscription end date: 2027-01-14

### Step 2 — Invoice History

Existing invoice records were retrieved.

**Findings:**

* September 2026 invoice: Present
* October 2026 invoice: Not found

### Step 3 — Missing Invoice Detection

A SQL LEFT JOIN query was executed to identify missing invoice records.

**Result:**

The October invoice returned `NULL` values for the invoice number and billing period.

### Step 4 — Additional Verification

A SQL NOT EXISTS query was executed.

**Result:**

The investigation confirmed that no October 2026 invoice record existed for the affected subscription.

## 3. Findings

The investigation established that:

1. The customer has a subscription marked as active.
2. A September 2026 invoice exists.
3. No October 2026 invoice record exists in the database.

These findings confirm a missing billing record.

## 4. Root Cause Analysis

The precise reason for the missing invoice has not been established.

Potential explanations include:

* A failed automated billing process.
* Incorrect invoice-generation configuration.
* A processing exception.
* An issue with the billing schedule.

Further investigation of billing rules and application logs would be required.

## 5. Recommended Actions

1. Verify the customer's billing schedule.
2. Review the automated invoice-generation process.
3. Check billing application logs for processing errors.
4. Confirm whether an October invoice should have been generated.
5. Escalate to the billing operations team if necessary.
6. Generate or restore the missing invoice only after authorization.
7. Confirm that the customer can access the invoice.

## 6. Resolution Status

**Investigation:** Completed
**Root cause:** Not conclusively confirmed
**Resolution:** Pending further investigation

No database records were modified.
