# INC-003 — Duplicate Customer Records

## Incident Information

**Incident ID:** INC-003
**Priority:** P3 — Low (simulated)
**Customer:** CloudBridge Ltd
**Category:** Data Quality / Customer Management
**Status:** Investigation Completed — Resolution Pending

## 1. Problem Description

CloudBridge Ltd reported that its company appeared twice in the SaaS customer management system.

The customer also reported that different users appeared to be associated with separate customer records.

The support investigation focused on identifying duplicate records and examining their relationships with other database entities.

## 2. Investigation

The investigation was performed using SQLite and DBeaver.

Four database tables were examined:

* `customers`
* `users`
* `subscriptions`
* `invoices`

### Step 1 — Duplicate Detection

A SQL query using `GROUP BY`, `COUNT(*)` and `HAVING` was executed to identify potential duplicate customer records.

**Findings:**

Two customer records were identified:

* Customer ID: 2
* Customer ID: 5

Both records share the same company name and email address.

### Step 2 — Associated User Investigation

A SQL LEFT JOIN query was executed to identify users associated with each customer record.

**Findings:**

| Customer ID | Associated User |
| ----------- | --------------- |
| 2           | James Wilson    |
| 5           | Sarah Johnson   |

The two customer records have different users associated with them.

### Step 3 — Subscription Investigation

The associated subscription records were examined.

**Findings:**

| Customer ID | Subscription ID | Status |
| ----------- | --------------- | ------ |
| 2           | 2               | Active |
| 5           | 5               | Active |

Both customer records have active subscriptions.

### Step 4 — Invoice Investigation

Invoice records were examined to identify potential billing dependencies.

**Findings:**

| Customer ID | Invoice Number | Amount    |
| ----------- | -------------- | --------- |
| 2           | INV-2026-002   | 99.00 GBP |
| 5           | INV-2026-004   | 99.00 GBP |

Both customer records have associated invoices.

## 3. Findings

The investigation confirmed that:

1. Two customer records share the same company name and email address.
2. Each record has a different associated user.
3. Each record has an active subscription.
4. Each record has an associated invoice.

The records are potential duplicates, but their business relationships must be reviewed before consolidation.

## 4. Root Cause Analysis

The exact cause of the duplicate records has not been established.

Possible explanations include:

* Duplicate customer registration.
* Data migration errors.
* Synchronization issues between systems.
* Missing duplicate-prevention controls.

Additional application logs and customer-registration history would be required to determine the root cause.

## 5. Recommended Actions

1. Confirm whether both records represent the same legal customer.
2. Identify the authoritative customer record.
3. Verify whether the subscriptions represent separate contracts.
4. Review the associated invoices for potential duplicate billing.
5. Determine how user relationships should be preserved.
6. Escalate the proposed consolidation to the authorized data management team.
7. Validate all relationships after any approved changes.

No customer records were deleted or modified during the investigation.
